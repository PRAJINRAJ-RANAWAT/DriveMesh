Unlink account

Unlink ?

DriveMesh will first copy every chunk stored on this account to your other accounts (evacuation), then remove its copies and forget the account. Nothing is lost, but it can take a while for large accounts.

If your remaining accounts can't hold two copies of every chunk, unlinking is refused.
