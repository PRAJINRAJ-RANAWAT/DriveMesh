How it works

Three steps from scattered storage to one durable drive.

1

Link your accounts

Connect the Google accounts you own with a least-privilege scope: DriveMesh can only touch the files it creates.

2

Split & encrypt

Every file is streamed in 8 MiB chunks, each sealed with AES-256-GCM under its own per-file key and bound to its position.

3

Replicate across accounts

Each chunk lands on two or more different accounts, chosen by free space and load. Background workers keep it that way.

Durability

What if an account dies?

Nothing you'd notice. Reads fall back to the other copy instantly, and DriveMesh rebuilds the missing copies on healthy accounts, the same idea that keeps HDFS clusters alive when a machine fails.

  1. 1Heartbeat missedThe account is marked unreachable; reads skip it immediately.
  2. 2Most endangered firstChunks down to a single copy are queued ahead of everything else.
  3. 3Copies rebuiltEach chunk is verified, then copied to an account that doesn't hold it yet.
